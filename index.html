Grammar Core: Present Perfect Progressive

We use this tense to connect the past and the present. It emphasizes the duration of an activity or the result of a recent continuous action.

The Anatomy of the Tense

Subject + HAVE/HAS + BEEN + Verb(-ing)

Use Case 1: Duration

An activity that started in the past and is still in progress now.

🧑‍💻☕

"Alex started coding at 9 AM. It is now 5 PM."

➡ He has been coding for 8 hours.

Use Case 2: Recent Result

An activity that recently stopped, but has a visible result now.

🏃‍♀️🥵

"Sarah is exhausted and sweating right now."

➡ Because she has been running.

Time Markers: For vs. Since

FOR

A duration/length of time

for 3 hours, for 10 years, for a long time

SINCE

A specific starting point in the past

since 1950, since 8 AM, since Monday
